OrderzHouse is a marketplace that brings clients and freelancers together for real projects.

FOR CLIENTS
• Post fixed-price or hourly projects and describe what you need.
• Review freelancers, offers, and applications in one place.
• Track project status from posting through delivery.
• Use payments and project tools designed for clear milestones.

FOR FREELANCERS
• Explore open projects and send competitive offers.
• Manage active work: applications, deliveries, and client communication.
• Get paid through the platform’s payment flows tied to your projects.
• Build your profile and grow repeat work with verified clients.

WHY ORDERZHOUSE
• Role-based experience for clients and freelancers.
• Structured project lifecycle: post → offers → work → delivery.
• Built for real collaboration—not generic chat-only gigs.

OrderzHouse helps you run serious freelance work with clarity from first brief to final payment.
